# Editorial Paper

Warm paper, black ink, serif headlines, one terracotta accent. An editorial
spread that moves — ideas argued like a magazine feature.

## Palette

- Background: warm paper (aged cream)
- Surface: slightly darker paper panels, torn-edge cards
- Primary ink: near-black ink
- Secondary ink: faded ink for captions and rules
- Main accent: terracotta — the one warm signal (underlines, stamps, seals)
- Secondary accent: deep olive — rare, for secondary marks

## Typography

High-contrast serif (Playfair-style) for headlines, grotesk for body, mono
for marginalia. Drop caps, pull quotes, footnote numbers.

## Texture

Paper grain, ink bleed on headlines, deckled edges, coffee-ring faint marks.
Print misregistration on the accent color (1-2px offset).

## Motifs

Marginalia, red-pen underlines, stamps ("VERIFIED", "DRAFT"), paper clips,
taped photos, footnote daggers, column rules. Objects dissolve into paper
dust on exit.

## Motion

Gentle: ink draws on (stroke reveal), paper slides with slight rotation,
dust particles on dissolves. Slow push-ins; hard cuts rare. Everything
feels printed, then alive.

## Composition

Magazine grid: headline top, two-column body, marginalia in the margins.
Generous whitespace — the paper is the luxury.

## Signature transition

Ink bleed / paper dissolve: the outgoing frame desaturates into paper
fibers and blows away as dust; the incoming frame inks itself on.

## Best for

Ideas pieces, manifestos, "the invisible" narratives, brand stories.

## Never

Neon anything; drop shadows; glossy UI; more than one accent.
