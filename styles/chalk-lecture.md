# Chalk Lecture

Dark board, chalk derivations. A great teacher thinking aloud —
one sentence narrated, one sentence written, the board filling
with the argument.

## Palette

- Background: dark slate board
- Surface: slightly lighter board panels
- Primary ink: chalk white
- Secondary ink: chalk gray for scaffolding
- Main accent: chalk yellow — the key result, circled
- Secondary accent: chalk pink/blue — secondary threads only

## Typography

Chalk-style serif or handwriting font for derivations, clean grotesk
for labels. Underline, circle, box, star — the teacher's markup.

## Texture

Chalk dust, smudges from erased mistakes (leave the ghosts — they
prove thinking happened), board grain.

## Motifs

Equations, diagrams, arrows, circled results, margin notes, the
occasional "wait, actually…" correction. A hand with chalk is the
only character needed.

## Motion

Write-on: strokes draw at speaking pace (~0.5s after the phrase, so
the ear leads). Camera wanders a board larger than the frame; ends
with a pull-back revealing the full derivation. Mistakes get
visibly corrected — strikethrough, rewrite.

## Composition

One board, infinite in implication: the viewport shows a section,
marginalia accumulates. Never a full clean board at the start —
build it live.

## Signature transition

Wipe and rewrite: a sleeve wipes a section of board (dust puff),
the new derivation writes itself in.

## Best for

Reasoning chains, math, "why" arguments, derivations, mechanisms
explained step by step.

## Never

A pre-filled board; digital smoothness; more than two chalk colors
per derivation.
