# Risograph

Misregistered print layers, visible grain, limited inks. Zine energy —
loud, imperfect, alive.

## Palette (2-3 inks only)

- Background: off-white paper stock
- Surface: solid ink blocks (one ink at 100%)
- Primary ink: darkest ink (usually black or navy)
- Secondary ink: same ink at 40-60% (halftone)
- Main accent: fluorescent pink or vivid green — the riso pop
- Secondary accent: a second bright ink, used rarely

## Typography

Bold grotesk, tightly tracked, often overprinted (two inks overlapping
the same glyph). Gig-poster hierarchy: huge headline, tiny details.

## Texture

Heavy grain, halftone dots on tints, misregistration offset (2-4px)
between ink layers, roller marks, paper speck.

## Motifs

Overprinted shapes, starbursts, halftone portraits, ticket stubs,
rubber-stamp marks. Ink layers visibly separate on motion.

## Motion

Layers slide against each other (misregistration animates: 0→4px drift),
starbursts pop with overshoot, halftones wipe on. Slight weave (1px
frame jitter) sells the print feel.

## Composition

Poster grid: massive type, cropped shapes bleeding off edges, dense
but organized. Bleed is a feature.

## Signature transition

Ink-layer slide: the new scene's ink layers slide in from different
edges at different speeds and snap into registration with a soft thump.

## Best for

Music-culture topics, drops, community stories, anything with zine
soul.

## Never

More than 3 inks; perfect registration; smooth gradients (use halftone
instead); pastel timidity.
