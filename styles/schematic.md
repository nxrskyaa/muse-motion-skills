# Schematic

Technical drawing on cream: precise linework, dimension callouts,
stamps. The mechanism explained like an engineer's plate.

## Palette

- Background: cream drafting paper
- Surface: white detail panels with ruled borders
- Primary ink: drafting black
- Secondary ink: cool gray for construction lines
- Main accent: drafting red — callouts, dimensions, warnings
- Secondary accent: steel blue — water/fluid/flow lines only

## Typography

Condensed grotesk for labels, mono for measurements and part numbers.
All caps, letterspaced. Title block bottom-right like a real drawing.

## Texture

Paper tooth, faint grid, coffee-free clean. Eraser smudges allowed
on revisions.

## Motifs

Cross-sections, exploded views, dimension lines with arrows, section
cuts, hatching, north arrows, revision stamps (REV A, REV B). Parts
labeled like a bill of materials.

## Motion

Draw-on: lines plot themselves (stroke-dashoffset), dimensions snap
in with ticks, exploded views separate along axes. The drawing
builds itself as the VO explains it — never fully formed before
its sentence.

## Composition

Plate layout: main view center, detail callouts around the margins,
title block anchored. Leader lines connect labels to parts.

## Signature transition

Redraw: the old plate's ink lifts (erased in a pass), construction
lines for the new plate plot themselves, then final ink draws over.

## Best for

Mechanisms, how-it-works, architecture deep-dives, teardowns.

## Never

Decorative illustration; unexplained decoration; color without a
drafting reason.
