# Art Deco

Gold on black, geometric luxury frames. The announcement as a
premiere — symmetrical, gleaming, deliberate.

## Palette

- Background: deep black (warm, not blue)
- Surface: black lacquer panels with gold hairlines
- Primary ink: warm white / champagne
- Secondary ink: muted gold for secondary lines
- Main accent: metallic gold — frames, rules, hero words
- Secondary accent: emerald or ruby — one jewel tone, rare

## Typography

Deco display (high-contrast geometric serif) for headlines,
letterspaced caps for labels. Symmetry in type blocks.

## Texture

Subtle gold-foil grain, sunburst engraving lines, lacquer sheen
on panels. No noise for its own sake.

## Motifs

Sunbursts, chevrons, stepped arches, fan motifs, symmetrical
frames, tickets and seals. Everything mirrored or radial.

## Motion

Stately: gold lines draw symmetrically from center, panels unfold
like curtains, sunbursts rotate slowly. Slow, confident — speed
would cheapen it.

## Composition

Bilateral symmetry, centered medallions, strong verticals. Frames
within frames. The subject enthroned at center.

## Signature transition

Curtain unfold: gold hairlines draw a deco frame, the old scene
dims inside it, the new scene rises from center with a sunburst.

## Best for

Launches, premium announcements, milestones, "the main event".

## Never

Asymmetry without intent; fast cuts; more than one jewel tone;
anything casual.
