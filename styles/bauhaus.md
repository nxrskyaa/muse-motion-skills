# Bauhaus

Geometric poster: circles, bars, and primary shapes composed like a 1923
exhibition print. Systems made visible through pure form.

## Palette

- Background: aged paper cream
- Surface: flat paper panels
- Primary ink: near-black
- Secondary ink: warm gray
- Main accent: primary red — circles, signals
- Secondary accent: primary blue + yellow — bars, triangles (geometric only)

## Typography

Geometric sans (Poppins/Futura-style), all caps, tracked wide for labels;
huge lowercase for statements. Vertical type as a compositional element.

## Texture

Light print grain, subtle paper mottle. Flat color — no gradients, no
shadows.

## Motifs

Circles, half-circles, bars, triangles, grids, diagonal rods. Figures
built from geometry (circle head, trapezoid torso, rod limbs) — abstract,
never cute.

## Motion

Mechanical and precise: rods rotate around pivots, color blocks cycle on
a beat grid, concentric arcs sweep. Motion is rhythmic, like a metronome —
everything lands on the grid.

## Composition

Asymmetric poster balance: one dominant circle or diagonal, type as
architecture. Strong horizontals (ground lines) anchor floating geometry.

## Signature transition

Geometric assembly: the new composition's shapes scale in from center
(circle → bars → type) while the old one disassembles outward.

## Best for

Systems, architecture, design topics, "how parts fit together".

## Never

Organic curves without geometric reason; gradients; drop shadows;
illustrative characters.
