# Cut Paper

Torn-paper collage with stop-motion jitter. Scissors-and-glue energy:
every element is a cutout, slightly imperfect, charmingly handmade.

## Palette

- Background: craft-board tone (warm gray or muted color)
- Surface: cutout papers in 3-4 flat hues
- Primary ink: dark ink for hand-lettered titles
- Secondary ink: pencil-gray annotations
- Main accent: one saturated cutout color (signal red or cobalt)
- Secondary accent: tape-beige / highlight yellow

## Typography

Hand-lettered display (or a rough marker font), pencil annotations in a
casual script. Letters can sit slightly rotated, like glued headlines.

## Texture

Torn edges (jagged alpha masks), visible tape strips, glue wrinkles,
paper fiber. Drop shadows are real — cutouts cast soft shadows on the
board.

## Motifs

Scissors, tape, string, pins, cutout figures with visible joints, paper
craters where pieces were removed. Hands entering frame to place pieces.

## Motion

Stop-motion feel: 8-12 fps stepping on object moves, 1-2px position
jitter ("boiling") on holds. Pieces drop in with a soft paper thump;
exits are lifted away by a hand or blown off.

## Composition

Collage layering: 3-5 depth layers, overlapping edges, tape at the
corners. Slight rotations (1-3°) everywhere — nothing perfectly aligned.

## Signature transition

Hand sweep: a hand (or paper arm) sweeps the old collage off-frame while
dropping the new pieces in, one every 2-3 frames.

## Best for

Playful explainers, histories, "how we got here" narratives.

## Never

Perfect alignment; smooth expo easing on paper (breaks the handmade
illusion); digital gloss.
