# Ukiyo-e

Woodblock waves and flat color bands. A journey told like a print:
layered planes, drifting mist, one great wave carrying the story.

## Palette

- Background: pale washi (warm white)
- Surface: flat color bands (sky, sea, land — each one flat tone)
- Primary ink: sumi black for key lines and text
- Secondary ink: gray-blue for distant planes
- Main accent: prussian blue — water, depth, movement
- Secondary accent: persimmon red — seals, sun, signals

## Typography

Vertical Japanese-style setting for display (or vertical Latin),
horizontal mincho-style serif for body. Red hanko seal as the mark.

## Texture

Woodgrain in flat areas, bokashi (gradation) only as hand-pulled
bands — never digital gradients. Deckled print edges.

## Motifs

Waves with claw foam, distant Fuji-like silhouettes, mist bands,
boats, bridges, birds as calligraphic strokes. Figures as flat
silhouettes.

## Motion

Slow drift: mist bands glide horizontally, waves cycle in loops,
distant planes parallax. Everything unhurried — the print breathes.
Cuts are rare; the camera travels.

## Composition

Layered planes: foreground / middle / distance, each a flat band.
Asymmetric balance, generous sky. The journey moves left to right.

## Signature transition

Wave sweep: a great wave rises from the bottom edge, foam claw
trailing, and washes the old scene away — the new scene revealed
in its wake.

## Best for

Journeys, flows, natural metaphors, "crossing" narratives.

## Never

Digital gradients; fast cuts; photorealistic anything; more than
two accents.
