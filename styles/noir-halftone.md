# Noir Halftone

Comic ink, halftone dots, hard shadows. High-contrast drama for
conflict, investigation, and stakes.

## Palette

- Background: newsprint off-white (or inverted: black for night scenes)
- Surface: ink-black panels
- Primary ink: black (or white when inverted)
- Secondary ink: halftone gray
- Main accent: blood red — used only for the critical reveal
- Secondary accent: none. Restraint is the style.

## Typography

Condensed bold for headlines, typewriter mono for case files. Sound
effects as giant onomatopoeia (KRAK, SNAP) — sparingly.

## Texture

Halftone dots (varying size for tone), ink splatter, rough brush
edges, newsprint grain.

## Motifs

Venetian-blind shadows, magnifying glasses, red string boards,
case-file stamps, silhouettes in doorways. Faces in shadow or
cropped — never fully lit.

## Motion

Hard cuts, snap zooms on reveals, halftone dots growing for
emphasis. Shadows crawl. The red accent appears exactly once per
video at the turning point — that's the rule.

## Composition

Dutch angles, extreme close-ups, heavy blacks occupying half the
frame. Panels like comic gutters for sequences.

## Signature transition

Ink slash: a brush stroke slashes diagonally across frame, the new
scene bleeds through the stroke.

## Best for

Conflict, investigations, security topics, "what went wrong"
postmortems.

## Never

The red accent twice; soft gradients; cheerful anything; fully-lit
faces.
